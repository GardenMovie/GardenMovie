## About Me

Hello! I'm Dody, a Computer Science undergraduate based in São Paulo, Brazil. EU citizen, fluent in English (C1) and Portuguese (C2).

Currently in my third year of a dual Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Science & Technology at the Federal University of ABC (UFABC). I completed an 8-month internship at InterAntar as a game developer (May 2025 – Jan 2026), where I shipped 3 production mobile applications and collaborated in an agile team to improve 18 applications.

I've also organized large-scale engineering events, leading 140+ volunteers and coordinating logistics for 200+ activities at SEUFABC.
